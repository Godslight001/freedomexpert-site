The Self-Discovery Intensive

This reflective program guides participants through a structured process of spiritual discovery, psychological healing, and purpose clarification.

4 Weeks Identity, Healing, and Purpose Alignment

Curriculum

Participants move through four stages of discovery, restoration, inner healing, and purpose synthesis.

Week 1

The Smallest Flicker

Identifying innate curiosity and the seed of potential placed within each individual.

Week 2

Vertical Alignment

Restoring relationship with God as the source of identity, knowledge, and worth.

Week 3

Psychological Barriers

Addressing internal narratives and childhood patterns that limit spiritual maturity.

Week 4

The Purpose Mandate

Synthesizing personal gifts, history, and resources into a clear personal mission.

Empowering believers to master their finances and live out their God-given mission through editorial excellence.
